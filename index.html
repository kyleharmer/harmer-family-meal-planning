Family Meal Playbook

Budget-minded dinners and lighter lunches built around oven, crockpot, griddle, and assembly meals. The main list avoids soups/stews until fall/winter and prioritizes meals with seven-ish ingredients or fewer.

Ingredient count is intentionally rough and assumes pantry basics like salt, pepper, oil, garlic powder, and butter do not count. Cost is estimated for a family meal using budget groceries/sale pricing and will vary by store.
